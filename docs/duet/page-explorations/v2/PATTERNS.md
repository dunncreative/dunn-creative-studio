19 Nov 2026. A short read of the references, turned into layout moves for the v2 pages.

- **Freeman's 42 reference page designs** in Dropbox `00_Brand-Brain/References/Website-Design-Style-Page-References` (home, services, work, case study, about, skills, journal, contact, pitch decks, products, cinematic, plus 27 single-service pages). - **Superside's pricing page** (read 19 Nov) for the on-demand / subscription model he mentioned.
- **Our own earlier explorations:** Ridge (picked), Type, Frame and the three lead pages, so v2 keeps what he already approved.

## Keep from Ridge (approved, don't re-decide)

- Palette A: white and greys, ink #0B0C0D, teal #1AA6AA as the only accent, Grey 600 #6B6E72 captions on light.
- Inter at 400/500, big light headlines with tight tracking, 17px body, sentence-case rounded buttons (about 10px), light sticky nav.
- Dark bands with the faint texture; one solid teal button per screen; client names as text, no logos.

## Patterns worth trying

### Structure and hierarchy
1. **Dark hero, then alternate light and dark bands.** The best reference home (example 2) runs hero, intro, capabilities, process, work, client names, close. Seven bands, not nine.
2. **Services before work on Home** (Freeman, 19 Nov). Services say what we do; work proves it right after.
3. **Eyebrow, headline, one line, action.** Every section opens the same way, so a skim reads as a table of contents.
4. **Fewer, ranked services.** 17 equal tiles read as a menu (the review's main critique of the services mockup). Five or six services, with one or two given more weight.

### Grids and modules
5. **Module cards in a row/column grid** for services: grey or white containers with a number, title, one line, tags and an arrow. Variants: even 3x2, a bento with one large and several small, and grouped sections of 3 or 4.
6. **Work in rhythm:** two featured across, three below, repeat. Varying the row size keeps a long grid from going flat.
7. **Full-bleed alternating rows** for work: image to the page edge, text column with project, brand, line and services used, flipping sides each row.
8. **Type-led index** (from Type and the reference work index): large project names in a ruled list with brand and discipline, a thumbnail on hover. Fast to scan, and strong when images are uneven.
9. **Filter chips** above work and services, a pattern in the reference work index.

### Type
10. **One weight jump per section:** 400 display headline, 500 card titles, 13px grey labels. No teal phrase in headlines (the review counted the tic in almost every mockup).
11. **Large numerals as structure** (01 to 05) only where order matters, like process steps.
12. Pairing to keep: Inter alone. A serif or mono accent was tried in Type and Frame and wasn't picked, so v2 stays single-family and gets contrast from size and weight.

### Pages
13. **Contact as steps:** one question per step was the best conversion pattern in the references. One v2 contact tries it.
14. **Case study:** challenge, approach, what we made, outcome, with a facts sidebar (client, deliverables, channels).
15. **Journal / Lab post:** a reading column of about 68 characters, author card, tags, related posts.
16. **Plans page (from Superside):** eyebrow and H1, three plan cards with a "best for" line, a use-case grid, FAQ, closing CTA. Superside has no step-by-step request flow, which leaves room for ours to be clearer.

## Avoid

- AI stock imagery shown as client work. Label AI-made images every time.
- Invented clients, numbers, quotes or prices. Prices stay off the site.
- Same seven sections on every service page.
- Small dense body copy.
