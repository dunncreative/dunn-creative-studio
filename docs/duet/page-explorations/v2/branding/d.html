Why branding

A better brand makes every other part of the business easier to strengthen.

Branding isn’t surface decoration. When the identity is unclear, websites drift, messaging gets inconsistent and marketing loses cohesion. A clear brand fixes that at the source, and turns scattered impressions into a system people recognize.
