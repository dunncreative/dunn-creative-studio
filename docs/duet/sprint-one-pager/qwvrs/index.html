In short

QWVRS is a small senior creative studio that sells brand, web, launch and AI work in fixed sprints. A Brand Sprint takes ten working days, a Web Sprint takes twenty, a Launch Sprint takes fifteen and an AI Team Sprint takes ten. Each has a fixed scope and one fixed price, agreed before kickoff. After launch, the same team can stay on through Ongoing, a monthly plan for design, social, SEO and AEO content.

Questions

What does a sprint cost?

Each sprint has one fixed price, listed on its page. Add-ons are priced too, so you know the total before you book.

How can a brand take ten days?

A questionnaire replaces workshops, decisions happen on live calls, a small senior team does the work, and AI handles the repetitive production so people spend their time on the creative calls.

How soon can we start?

Sprints start on Mondays. Open weeks are shown on every page, and booking a call holds yours for a few days.

Who does the work?

A creative director leads every sprint, with a small senior bench for web, motion and copy. No juniors and no handoffs.

What if we need something that isn't on the menu?

Tell us on the call. If it fits a sprint plus add-ons, we'll price it on the spot. If it doesn't, we'll say so and point you somewhere good.
