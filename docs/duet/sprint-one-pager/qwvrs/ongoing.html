Ask about Ongoing

Is it right for you?

Ongoing works best after a sprint, when the brand and site are in place and you need a steady output.

A good fit if

  • You've launched and need steady content
  • You'd rather not hire a full in-house team yet
  • You can plan a month ahead
  • You want social and search handled together

Not a fit if

  • You need a brand or site first; start with a sprint
  • You need same-day turnaround on everything
  • You want paid media buying managed
  • You only need one or two pieces

Questions

What does Ongoing cost?

Plans start at the monthly price shown. The exact plan is set on a call, based on your channels and how much you need each month.

What's the minimum term?

Three months, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

Do we need to do a sprint first?

Not always, but it helps. Clients who start with a sprint get going faster because the team already knows the brand.

What counts as a standard request?

Things like a social graphic, a resized ad or a page update. Larger pieces are planned into the month.

Do you run paid ads?

We make the ads. Buying and managing paid media stays with you or your media partner.
