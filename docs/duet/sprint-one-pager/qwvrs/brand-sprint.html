Is it right for you?

A sprint works when decisions can happen fast. If that's hard right now, a slower project may suit you better.

A good fit if

  • You're launching or relaunching in the next three months
  • The people who decide can join a one-hour call
  • You want a system your team can run without us
  • Your positioning is clear, or you add a strategy day

Not a fit if

  • You need research across many stakeholders first
  • Every decision needs a committee's sign-off
  • You'd like ten logo options to choose from
  • You're rebranding a large family of products at once

Questions

What does a Brand Sprint cost?

One fixed price, shown above, for everything listed on this page. Add-ons are priced separately and agreed before kickoff.

Is ten working days really enough?

Yes, for a focused brand. The questionnaire does the discovery, you choose on live calls, and nothing sits waiting for feedback.

What do you need from us?

The questionnaire, about twenty minutes, and the people who decide on four calls of an hour or less.

What if we don't like either direction?

It's rare, because the questionnaire sets the brief. If it happens, we take what worked from both and show a third route by day five.

Do we own everything?

Yes. Final files, source files and rights to the finished identity are yours at handover.

Can we add a website?

Book Brand and Web. The Web Sprint starts the week after handover, so you're live in about seven weeks.
