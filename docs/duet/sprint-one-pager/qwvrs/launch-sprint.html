Is it right for you?

A Launch Sprint suits one clear moment. For a steady flow of content, look at Ongoing.

A good fit if

  • You have a launch, raise or event in the next six weeks
  • You have a brand system, or book Brand first
  • There's one message the launch must land
  • You can review on four short calls

Not a fit if

  • You need a multi-day shoot with a large crew
  • You want a steady flow of content every month
  • The piece needs broadcast delivery specs
  • The message is still being decided

Questions

What does a Launch Sprint cost?

One fixed price for the hero piece, cutdowns and sets shown above. A shoot day is an add-on.

Do you film, or use AI?

Both, depending on the idea. We tell you at kickoff which shots are filmed, sourced or AI-assisted, and every frame is art directed.

Who writes the script?

We do, from your questionnaire. You approve it on day four.

Can we use the work in paid ads?

Yes. Music and footage are licensed for organic and paid use on the channels we agree at kickoff.

How many rounds of changes?

Two at each stage: script, storyboard and cut. You approve the storyboard before anything is animated.

Can it be 3D?

Yes. 3D adds about a week and is priced as an add-on, agreed before kickoff.

Do we need a new brand first?

No. We make it in the brand you have. If you're rebranding too, the Launch Sprint can follow a Brand Sprint.
