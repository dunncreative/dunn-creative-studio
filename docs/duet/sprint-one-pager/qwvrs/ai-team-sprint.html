Is it right for you?

This sprint suits teams already making content every week who want it faster and more on-brand.

A good fit if

  • A team of 2 to 15 makes content every week
  • You have a brand, or book Brand first

Not a fit if

Questions

Which AI tools do you work with?

Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, plus common image tools. We set up whichever your team already uses, or help you pick one.

Do we need paid accounts?

Yes, team accounts on your chosen tool. We set everything up inside your own accounts.

What about our data?

Everything lives in your accounts under your settings. We walk through the data and sharing options in the team session.

Will this replace our designers?

No. It takes the repetitive first drafts off their plate so they spend more time on the work that needs them.

What happens after 30 days?

We check in, see what's used, and tune the templates. A quarterly tune-up is available as an add-on.

Can you make the content for us instead?

Yes, that's Ongoing. Some teams do both: AI Team Sprint for daily jobs, Ongoing for the bigger pieces.
