From first call to handover

Every sprint follows the same path. Only the number of days in the middle changes.

  • Before
    Book a 30-minute call
    We check fit, choose the sprint and add-ons, and hold your start week.
  • Before
    Fill in the questionnaire
    About twenty minutes: goals, audience, competitors, what you like and what you don't.
  • Day 1
    Kickoff
    One hour. We confirm scope, lock your menu choices and set every review date.
  • During
    Live reviews
    Work is shown on short calls. You decide there and then, and we keep moving.
  • Last day
    Handover
    Files, guidelines and a recorded walkthrough, all in your name.
  • After
    Ongoing, if you want it
    The same team stays on monthly for design, motion, social and search.

Questions

Where are you based?

We work remotely with teams anywhere. Calls are scheduled in your time zone.

How do payments work?

Half when you book your start week, half at handover. Ongoing is billed monthly.

Can a sprint pause?

Once started, a sprint runs to its dates. If you can't make a review, we move it within the same week.

What happens after handover?

You have everything. Web Sprints include 30 days of fixes, and Ongoing keeps the same team on monthly.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it with your brief and we'll return it before the call.
