FAQ

Straight answers.

What does a Brand Sprint cost?

From $X, fixed. The price on the page is the price you pay, for the scope on the page.

What does a Web Sprint cost?

A landing page is from $X and a site of up to five pages is from $X. Larger sites get a fixed quote after an Audit.

What if we aren't ready for a sprint?

Start with the Audit. It is from $X, takes a week, and the fee comes off a sprint if you go ahead within 30 days.

Who does the work?

Christopher Dunn directs every sprint and is on every call. AI speeds up drafts and production; every piece is directed and finished by hand, and we tell you what was AI-assisted.

What can make a sprint run late?

Decisions. That is why work is shown and decided on live calls, and why we ask for one person who can say yes.

What happens after the sprint?

You own everything and can run it yourself. Many teams move to the Creative Retainer for ongoing work.

Do you work with clients outside Jackson Hole?

Yes. The studio is in Jackson Hole, Wyoming, and every call is on video, so you can be anywhere.
