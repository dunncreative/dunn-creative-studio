FAQ

Straight answers.

What does a sprint cost?
To come: sprint prices

Every sprint has a fixed price for the scope on the page. Larger sites get a fixed quote after an Audit.

What if we aren’t ready for a sprint?
Start with the free Audit. A sprint works best with positioning you can state in a sentence, one person who can say yes, and a launch, raise or season with a real date.
Who does the work?
Christopher Dunn, owner and creative director, directs every sprint and is on every call. He brings 20+ years in creative direction, brand, web and content, including senior creative roles at Teton Gravity Research and film campaigns built around release dates. AI speeds up drafts and production; every piece is directed and finished by hand, and we tell you what was AI-assisted.
How is the scope set?
You pick the pieces at kickoff, four to six from a menu for a Brand Sprint. The scope is fixed the moment you pick, so day 10 stays day 10.
What can make a sprint run late?
Decisions. Work is shown and decided on live calls, not in email rounds, and we ask for one person who can say yes. Sign-off by committee is not a fit yet.
What happens after the sprint?
You own everything and can run it yourself. Many teams move to the Creative Retainer for ongoing work.
Do you work with clients outside Jackson?
Yes. The studio is in Jackson, WY, and every call is on video, so you can be anywhere.
