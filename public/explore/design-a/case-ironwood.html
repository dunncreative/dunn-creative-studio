The project

Industry

Brand words
Strong, Authentic, Premium, Enduring

Ironwood Homes is a custom home builder and general contractor based in Afton, Wyoming, building new homes and remodels across Star Valley with modern ranch design, honest materials and a real respect for each client’s budget. The team came to us with a strong visual concept: industrial, rustic, hand-forged and unmistakably Western. Our job was to turn it into a professional identity system that matches the homes behind the name. We built a flexible logo system around a primary vertical logo, with a horizontal lockup, an IH monogram and a circular badge, so it holds up at any size on any surface. Every color comes from the materials Ironwood builds with (iron, weathered steel, burnished bronze, timber and parchment), set in Crimson Pro and Work Sans. A quick-reference brand guide and production-ready files keep every touchpoint consistent, from business cards and letterhead to signage, trucks and a stitched hat.
