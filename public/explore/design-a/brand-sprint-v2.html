Is it right for you?

Fast decisions, fast brand.

A sprint works when decisions can happen quickly. If that’s hard right now, a slower project may suit you better.

A good fit if

  • ✓You’re launching or relaunching in the next three months
  • ✓The people who decide can join a one-hour call
  • ✓You want a system your team can run without us
  • ✓Your positioning is clear, or you add a strategy day

Not a fit if

  • —You need research across many stakeholders first
  • —Every decision needs a committee’s sign-off
  • —You’d like ten logo options to choose from
  • —You’re rebranding a large family of products at once

Questions

Good questions.

What does a Brand Sprint cost?+

One fixed price for everything on this page, agreed before kickoff. Add-ons are priced separately. Prices to come.

Is ten working days really enough?+

Yes, for a focused brand. The questionnaire does the discovery, you decide on live calls, and nothing sits waiting for feedback.

What do you need from us?+

The questionnaire, about twenty minutes, and the people who decide on short calls of an hour or less.

What if we don’t like either direction?+

It’s rare, because the questionnaire sets the brief. If it happens, we take what worked from both and show a third route by day 5.

Do we own everything?+

Yes. Final files, source files and rights to the finished identity are yours at handover.

Can we add a website?+

Yes. The Web Sprint can start the week after handover, so you can be live with a new brand and site in about seven weeks.
